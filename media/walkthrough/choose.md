By default the status bar shows your context window, your weekly limit, and
a focus estimate:

`🔥🔥 ctx 74% · 7d 62% · focus 58%`

You can add your 5-hour session limit, change which number drives the colour,
pick which parts of the editor tint, or turn colours off entirely and keep just
the status bar indicator.

Hovering the status bar always shows everything, whatever you choose to display.
